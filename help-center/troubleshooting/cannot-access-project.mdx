---
title: Cannot access project
description: Fix access issues when joining a WRLD project.
---

## Problem
- You see "Access Denied" when visiting a project link
- Can't view team docs

## Solution
<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your invitation">
    Ensure you've accepted the invite email from WRLD. Check the spam folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask an admin">
    An existing admin can add your email under Project > Members.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign out and sign in again">
    Sign out of WRLD, then sign in again to refresh your access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">Still having trouble? Email support@wrld.com for help.</Callout>
