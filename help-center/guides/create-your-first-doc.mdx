## Quick Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your project">
    Go to your WRLD dashboard and select your project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new doc">
    Click **Add Page** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write content">
    Enter your doc title and add content using the editor. Format with headings, lists, and code blocks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish** to make your doc visible to your team.
  </Step>
</Steps>
